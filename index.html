Before you contact support

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Aevia version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful when appropriate.

Accounts

The current release of Aevia does not require an account for its core features.

Calendar

Aevia may request Calendar access to understand your schedule. External Calendar events are treated as read-only inside Aevia.

Sleep & Energy

If you choose to grant supported Health access, Aevia may use sleep information on your device to provide planning context and energy-related insights.

Cloud AI in the current release

Cloud GPT and Claude functionality are disabled in the current release build.
